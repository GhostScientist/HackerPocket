# HackerWatch 3.0 — App Store "What's New"

HackerWatch 3.0 is a ground-up redesign built for quick, purposeful reading from your wrist.

WHAT'S NEW
• Six feeds: Top, New, Best, Ask HN, Show HN, and Jobs. Your last feed is remembered.
• Search Hacker News with dictation or Scribble. Recent searches are saved.
• Works offline. Stories and comments are cached, so the app opens instantly and updates in the background.
• Upvote stories and comments when you're logged in.
• Save stories for later. Your Saved list works without a connection.
• Read history dims stories you've already seen, and you can clear it anytime.
• Pocket Briefing: an optional five-story snapshot with a clear finish line.
• Long comment threads remember where you left off.
• Handoff articles to your iPhone to keep reading on a bigger screen.
• Tap the rectangular complication to jump straight to the story it shows.
• Subtle haptics for refresh, replies, and votes.

IMPROVED
• Cleaner, more readable layouts that lead with the headline. The story list is no longer covered by floating buttons; everything else lives behind the More button in the top-left corner.
• Story pages show the source as a subtle subheadline, put the post text first, and keep Comments, Read Article, and Save one tap away.
• Comments show author and time on one line with easy-to-tap actions.
• The complication shows the headline and how fresh it is.

FIXED
• Stories now appear in Hacker News order instead of a random order.
• Comment threads read top to bottom in the order Hacker News ranks them.
• Job posts open correctly.
• Losing your connection shows a clear message with a retry instead of a blank screen.
• Quotes, slashes, and other special characters in comments display correctly.

HackerWatch is free, has no ads, and collects no data.
